# 2026-10-31 · Philolog: a daily philosophy nugget on e-paper

## Goal

Turn the board into a daily philosophy companion. Each morning it shows a quote from the 30 French nuggets in my [philolog](https://github.com/slevin48/philolog) knowledge base, and the side buttons reveal the rest of the nugget.

Code and build steps: [projects/02-philolog](../projects/02-philolog/).

## What I built

- **08:00 every day:** the board wakes, draws the day's quote and author, and goes back to deep sleep. The e-paper keeps the image with no power.
- **BOOT or PWR:** turns to the next page: title and explanation, *À essayer*, source. After the last page, or 2 minutes idle, it goes back to the quote.
- **Nuggets:** generated into a C header from `nuggets.fr.md` at build time and not committed, because philolog is private.
- **French text:** drawn with u8g2's Latin-1 fonts. The generator turns `œ`, `—` and `…` into `oe`, `-` and `...`, and adds non-breaking spaces so `?`, `:` and `»` never start a line.

## What went wrong (and the fixes)

1. **The clock was stuck at the build time.** The first version slept for 14 h instead of 12 h. The cause: reading the RTC failed, so every wake-up fell back to `__DATE__`/`__TIME__`.
2. **The RTC didn't answer on I2C (`short read`).** Waveshare's examples switch on the audio rail (GPIO 42) before talking to the RTC, because that rail powers the I2C bus. I added the same, switched off during sleep.
3. **The RTC held 2056-01-01 with a "valid" flag.** It was a leftover from the factory firmware, and my "year ≥ 2026" check accepted it. RTC time must now fall between the build date and 20 years after it.
4. **Debugging a sleeping board is awkward.** The USB port disappears in deep sleep. I wrote small scripts that wait for the port to come back, flash, and send serial commands (`R` dumps the RTC, `T<epoch>` sets it).

## Verified on the board

- The buttons wake it from deep sleep and page through Jour 1 (quote plus 4 detail pages), then back to the quote.
- After a reboot and after a real deep sleep, the RTC reads within about 2 s of my Mac's clock.
- It goes back to sleep with the quote on screen, set to wake at 08:00.
